02 / About

Close to the hardware.
Clear about the human.

I am an embedded systems engineer in Malmö, working across the layers that make physical products dependable: electronics, real-time software, communication, control, and operator experience.

My background in robotics and control software has taken me through surgical robots, medical devices, and consumer electronics. I care about robust behavior, readable systems, and the details that help a team ship with confidence.

Working principle “Know thyself and nothing in excess.”
